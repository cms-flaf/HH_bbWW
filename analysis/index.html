    Running the analysis¶

    The pipeline — anaTuples, observables, histograms, plots — is the standard FLAF chain; follow the FLAF full-workflow walkthrough for the commands. This page collects what is specific to HH→bb̄WW.

    ERA=Run3_2022
VER=dev

law run FLAF.Analysis.tasks.HistPlotTask --era $ERA --version $VER --workflow local --test 1000

    The b-tag-shape cache runs first¶

    HH→bb̄WW computes b-tag shape weights in a dedicated caching stage, AnalysisCacheTask (aggregated by AnalysisCacheAggregationTask), which LAW runs automatically before histogramming — see FLAF → Task reference.

    Budget time for the cache on a cold start

    On a cold cache AnalysisCacheTask is slow (roughly an hour per branch), even for simple variables. When iterating, reuse an existing cache instead of recomputing it, using a per-task version override, e.g. --AnalysisCacheTask-version <existing> --AnalysisCacheAggregationTask-version <existing>.

    Mass reconstruction: DeepHME¶

    The HH mass is reconstructed with DeepHME (the bb̄WW counterpart to SVfit in bb̄ττ). It is part of the observable computation and requires no special command — it runs as part of the standard producer chain.

    DeepHME and the DNN producers run their ONNX models with one thread per session (FLAF.Common.OnnxSession), so their outputs do not depend on the core count of the batch node. They still differ in the last digits between x86_64-v3 and x86_64-v4 nodes: compare them with a tolerance (NN outputs and the CPU of the node).

    Stored gen-level t̄t information¶

    Processes with genInfo: [ TT ] store, next to the usual observables:

    Column Content Read by
    TTInfo_nLeptonicW number of leptonically decaying W bosons (0, 1 or 2; τ counts as leptonic) — (t̄t is not stitched: TT uses only the three decay-channel samples)
    genTop_{pt,eta,phi,mass} last-copy top and anti-top, in this order top-pT reweighting at AnaTupleFileTask (genTop_pt; configured for Run3_2022–2023BPix)
    genTop_b_{pt,eta,phi}, genTop_lep_{pt,eta,phi,mass} the b quark and the W's charged lepton of each top —
    genTop_lep_gen_kind GenLepton::Kind of that lepton, -1 for a hadronic W —

    TTInfo_* columns must stay scalars: the anaTuple stores all columns that share a prefix as one collection, so an array under TTInfo_ would turn the count into an array as well.

    Corrections in Run3_2024 and later¶

    config/Run3_2024/global.yaml (and 2025, 2026) inherits the analysis-wide corrections: block through the anchor *corrections_default and overrides only btag (UParTAK4, no shape calibration yet) and dy_hhbbtautau. A correction added to config/global.yaml — the shape weights pu, parton_shower, top_pt, pdf and qcd_scale included — therefore applies to those eras too; an entry that has to differ there is overridden as a whole.

    Resonant mass grid¶

    The bb̄WW signal is GluGluTo{Radion,BulkGraviton} at 40 mass points from 250 GeV to 5 TeV in each of Run3_2022 through Run3_2023BPix, with the single-lepton (2B2JLNu) and dilepton (2B2L2Nu) final states as separate datasets. In 2023 and 2023BPix the whole grid comes from the custom production (DSProd, read from private storage with fs_nanoAOD: + dirName:); in 2022 and 2022EE 18 points per final state do and the other 22 are central Run3Summer22 datasets. The central X→YH samples are not selected. The bb̄ττ signal (2B2Tau, processes GluGluTo{Radion,BulkGraviton}_bbTauTau) has the same 40 masses: in 2023 and 2023BPix 29 points per spin come from the custom production and 11 are central, in 2022 and 2022EE 7 per spin come from the custom production and 33 are central. Run3_Model carries every hypothesis through anaTuple and histogram production; a fit picks one later, in the datacard. See the physics model.

    Two networks consume that grid, and they do not cover the same part of it:

    • the dilepton DNN is parametric — one model per fold, evaluated at whatever masses DNN.columns in config/global.yaml asks for. It is configured over the produced points up to 1.5 TeV, and the datacard param_values in config/Datacards/DL/dl_spin{0,2}.yaml follow the same 25 points. The four lowest of them, 250 to 280 GeV, are below the lowest signal_mass_points of the models currently in config/DNN/DoubleLepton_Parametric_*_v1, so those scores extrapolate and stay provisional until the network is retrained over this grid.
    • the single-lepton two-stage DNN has a binary stage that is parametric too, evaluated at the TwoStageDNN.masses in config/global.yaml: the 30 produced points from 250 GeV to 2 TeV. Its multiclass stage does not depend on the mass, so neither do the SL signal and control regions (see below). The SL cards (config/Datacards/SL/) fit SR_SL alone, in the HME window of each mass (SR_SL_HMESub_m<MX>), with the data-driven Fakes as a background (Single-lepton fakes) and the DL preprocess step; no TT or W+jets control region is fitted, as they have no fake estimate. Expected limits only; they are not in StatInference.cards (they read the SL production, see Statistical inference); the earlier single-file SL card, config/Datacards/x_hh_bbww_SL_run3.yaml, is kept for reference only and still reads the per-mass regions SR_SL_M${MX} that no longer exist.

    Every mass point named in a variables: list also needs its binning in config/plot/histograms.yaml — a variable with no matching entry there stops HistProducerFromNTuple with a KeyError.

    Single-lepton two-stage DNN¶

    The TwoStageDNN payload producer (TwoStageDNNProducer in Analysis/DNN_Application.py, configured under payload_producers in config/global.yaml) applies two networks per category (boosted, resolved) and fold. The models and their feature lists are in config/DNN/SingleLepton_AllMassMulticlass_ParametricBinary/: <category>/multiclass_nParity<p>.onnx and <category>/parametric_binary_nParity<p>.onnx, next to {multiclass,binary}_<category>_dnn_config.yaml. Model p is applied to the events with event % 4 == (p + 3) % 4.

    1. Multiclass, one model for all masses. Its 7 outputs, in the order of class_names (Signal, TT, ST, WJets, DY, H, VV), are stored as TwoStageDNN_<class>. In Analysis/hh_bbww.py the class with the highest score selects the SL region: SR_SL for Signal, CR_SL_<class> for the others. The regions are built only when one of them is in the active flavour's regions (see Regions and sub-regions).
    2. Binary, parametrized by the resonance mass, which is the last input column. The producer evaluates it at each of TwoStageDNN.masses, filling that column with the hypothesis mass in GeV (the input normalization is part of the ONNX graph), and stores TwoStageDNN_M<mass>_binary. The last entry of the binary feature list must be mass_feature (X_mass), or the producer stops; the X_mass branch of the event is not read.

    What the models return and what is stored is configured per network in multiclass_output and binary_output:

    Key Meaning
    num_nodes number of model outputs, checked when the model runs (7 multiclass, 2 binary)
    type what the model returns: logit or probability
    activation what turns its logits into probabilities: softmax or sigmoid
    save_as what is stored: logit (raw), probability, or recomputed_logit
    signal_node binary model with more than one output: the signal output (1; 0 is background)

    The current models return raw logits. As configured (config/global.yaml), the binary network stores the raw logit z1 of its signal node (save_as: logit) and the multiclass network the softmax probability of each class (save_as: probability). recomputed_logit would store log(p / (1 − p)) of the softmax probability of each output, with p clipped to [logit_clip_eps, 1 − logit_clip_eps] (±23.03 for 1.0e-10); for the 2-output binary model that is the logit difference z1 − z0. The raw signal logit z1 has no absolute scale (softmax does not change when the same constant is added to both outputs) and no bound: the binary DNN entries of config/plot/histograms.yaml use [−23.5, 23.5] (94 bins in 1D, 47 on the DNN axis of the plots against DeepHME), and scores outside it are in the under- and overflow, which plots drop. The multiclass entries use the same axis, so their probabilities fill its bins [0, 0.5), [0.5, 1) and, for p = 1, [1, 1.5). Storing raw logits cannot be configured for a model that returns probabilities.

    An event that no fold scores stops the producer. A non-finite model output only prints a warning, with the event numbers, and is stored as it is.

    Columns taken from the central tree¶

    config/global.yaml lists in anaTuple_shift_invariant_columns the anaTuple columns that no systematic shift changes: event numbers and dataset metadata, generator weights, the pileup, parton- shower, PDF, scale and top-pT weights, pileup truth, the LHE record and the generator truth of the signal (gen*) and of t̄t (TTInfo_*, genTop_*). FLAF stores them in the central tree only and fills them in for events that only a shift selected, after checking that every variation agrees.

    • Only event-level generator quantities belong there. Generator information attached to a reconstructed object — lepN_gen_*, centralJet_matchedGenJet_*, the jet flavour labels — follows the selected object and changes under JES, JER and the lepton scales; the fuse step stops with Column '…' is declared shift-invariant but differs in … if one is listed.
    • HLT_* is left out: Analysis/hh_bbww.py defines a trigger column that GetColumnNames() does not list, and a column taken from the central tree is not listed there.
    • Changing the list changes the anaTuple layout, so it goes with a new anaTuple production.

    Categories¶

    HH→bb̄WW is analysed in a boosted category (a large-radius jet, high-p_T HH topologies) and two resolved ones, res2b (both b-jet candidates b-tagged) and recovery (only the candidate with the higher b-tag score b-tagged); inclusive holds every boosted and resolved event, resolved events without a b-tagged candidate included (Analysis/hh_bbww.py, categories in config/global.yaml). Category and channel selection is driven by config/global.yaml; narrow or extend it there or via your user_custom.yaml.

    Regions and sub-regions¶

    The regions come from the active histTuple flavour (histTuple_flavor), not from a list you set. The analysis' booking plugin (Analysis/hist_booking.py, named by hist_booking_module in config/global.yaml; FLAF's hook is described in its Booking plugin reference) fills QCDRegions, the list custom_regions names, from the flavour's regions: and the base regions of its sub_regions:. Everything that reads QCDRegions (createKeyFilterDict, histTuple_selectors, the plots) gets them from there.

    • To change the regions, select another flavour or edit the flavour's regions: in config/global.yaml. Setting QCDRegions in a user_custom.yaml has no effect: the plugin overwrites it.
    • Sub-regions (e.g. HMESub of Signal_Extraction_DL_window): a base region restricted by a cut per model point, read from a cuts file (config/sub_regions/hme_DL_v2610.yaml). They are filled in HistFromNtupleProducer, as keys of their own (<channel>/SR_HMESub_m<MX>/<category>), with the variables of their model point (signal_params in config/plot/histograms.yaml; variables without signal_params go in every point). The plugin's docstring describes the cut forms. An entry's name is letters and digits only: Analysis/EqualSignalBinning.py maps <base>_<name>_m<MX> back to its base region by the last two underscores.
    • Channels: an entry with channels: [e, mu] books its sub-region keys in these channels only.
    • Variables: an entry with variables: [ "TwoStageDNN_M{mass}_binary" ] books only these variables in its sub-regions ({<parameter>} replaced by the model point's value); each must be a variable of the flavour that belongs to the point. The other variables stay in the base regions.
    • Sidebands: an entry with sidebands: books, instead of its cuts file's window, the sidebands of that window per model point, category and channel: up to count per side, nearest first, each ending at the nearest bin edge at least min_width beyond the previous one, starting at the window edge or gap beyond it. The first sideband that would leave [low_limit, high_limit] ends the side, so a side keeps the sidebands that fit, possibly none. They are named after the entry with L<i> below and H<i> above the window, i = 1 the nearest (SR_SL_HMESBL1_m300). A sideband's cut is the window's cut with the window interval replaced, so other parts of the cut (e.g. a DNN cut) apply to it too; as any bin-form interval, a sideband ending at the last bin edge takes the overflow.
    • Single lepton (Signal_Extraction_SL_window): the binary DNN of mass MX (TwoStageDNN_M<MX>_binary, booked at its own mass only) in e and μ, in the SL HME window of MX (config/sub_regions/hme_SL_v2610.yaml, the [16, 84] % signal window per category, <REG>_HMESub_m<MX>) of SR_SL, the six CR_SL_* and the anti-isolated SR_SL_Fakeable, and in up to two sidebands of ≥ 50 GeV on each side of the window (<REG>_HMESB{L2,L1,H1,H2}_m<MX>) of SR_SL and SR_SL_Fakeable, as 1D histograms instead of the 2D binary_DNN_m<MX>_vs_HME. The single-lepton fit reads the windows of SR_SL; the fake-lepton estimate reads the sidebands of SR_SL and the window and sidebands of SR_SL_Fakeable; the CR_SL_* windows are booked but feed neither. A side keeps the sidebands within [240, 2000] GeV, 240 GeV being the DeepHME turn-on: at the lowest masses the lower side has one sideband or none. DeepHME_mass is booked in the base regions only. No DNN cut is applied in these sub-regions. The sidebands follow the windows of the cuts file per category: a new window file changes them with it. The CI flavour books the same sub-regions at MX = 300 from a window file of its own (config/sub_regions/ci_sl_hme.yaml, whose windows leave room for no, one and two lower sidebands), and books SR_SL_Fakeable: every base region of a flavour's sub-regions must be in its regions:, or the plots of that region find no histograms.

    Single-lepton fakes¶

    Events with a non-prompt or misidentified lepton in the single-lepton signal region are estimated from data: the process Fakes ("Non-prompt leptons", config/processes.yaml, Analysis/SL_fakes.py), a data-driven process of FLAF listed among the backgrounds of Run3_Model, Run3_Model_DYSplit and TestModel. It has no datasets: HistMergerTask computes it after every process is merged, in the merged file of each TwoStageDNN_M<MX>_binary of Signal_Extraction_SL_window (the HME sub-regions of Regions and sub-regions), when data was merged and the merge is not restricted with --process or --dataset. Other variables, flavours and binary DNNs without SL windows (1600–2000 GeV) get none.

    In the SL window SR_SL_HMESub_m<MX>, per channel (e, μ) and category (res2b, recovery, boosted), Fakes = max(f, 0) × TT bin by bin, with f the excess of data over the simulation in units of TT,

    f = (D − O) / T − 1      D data, O the simulated backgrounds other than TT, T TT,

    measured without the data of the isolated window, separately below and from the mass's split of the DNN (two parts, each applied to the TT bins of its part). Two schemes (scheme per mass):

    • H: f from the isolated HME sidebands, SR_SL_HMESB{L2,L1}_m<MX> pooled and SR_SL_HMESB{H1,H2}_m<MX> pooled, interpolated linearly in HME to the window at the middle of each region's HME interval;
    • V: f(isolated sidebands) / f(anti-isolated sidebands) × f(anti-isolated window), the anti-isolated regions being SR_SL_Fakeable_HMESB*_m<MX> and SR_SL_Fakeable_HMESub_m<MX>; f = 0 where the isolated sidebands or the anti-isolated window show no excess.

    Configuration (the analysts' choice): V up to 400 GeV, where the lower sidebands are missing or sit on the DeepHME turn-on, H above; the split per mass (the cuts of an earlier study, moved to the nearest 0.5 edge of the DNN axis; −3.5 at 1200, 1300 and 1500 GeV, as at 1400 GeV, where the study's cuts left the low part of the sidebands without TT). Every mass of config/sub_regions/hme_SL_v2610.yaml needs a split (a bin edge of the binary DNN) and a scheme, and every physics model listing Fakes a tt_process (test/test_sl_fakes.py checks them, and that the scheme's regions exist in every cell). A scheme whose regions a cell lacks is a configuration error, and so is a split that leaves one part with too little TT (min_tt) in a region group of every cell of a mass while the other part is determined in some cell: the merge stops rather than give no Fakes to the whole mass.

    Uncertainties, each in its part of the DNN: FakesStatLow/High (f shifted by its statistical error, propagated to first order from the yields of the regions; Up and Down are max(max(f, 0) ± σf, 0) × TT; per channel, category and era) and FakesMethodLow/High (max(f, 0) ± |max(fother, 0) − max(f, 0)| with the other scheme, per channel and category, correlated across eras; no shift where the other scheme's regions are missing or it is not determined). The members of the theory uncertainties (QCD scale, PDF) get no Fakes variation: FLAF estimates data-driven processes for the other sources only, so the sum of eras and the datacards take the Fakes nominal there. The nominal decides whether f is determined (at least min_tt weighted TT events in each DNN part of each region group and, for V, an excess in the anti-isolated sidebands): where it is not, Fakes = 0 in that cell (both parts, with zero variations and nuisances, so that a sum of eras and the datacards find the process everywhere), with a message; that era then adds nothing to the cell in a sum of eras. Where a variation leaves f undetermined in a part (no TT in a region group where the nominal had enough or, for V, no excess in the anti-isolated sidebands), that part keeps the nominal f, applied to the varied TT, with a message naming the cell and the variation. The variations of the simulation enter through D − O and T; data stay nominal.

    Blinding: the estimator never reads the data of the isolated window. The merged file holds them, as FLAF merges data in every region; the sub-regions are not plotted. The merge prints, per cell, the scheme used or that the cell is not estimated, and no number; in V, "not determined" with enough TT means that the anti-isolated sidebands show no excess in a part. Fakes appear on no control plot yet; the SL datacards include them (config/Datacards/SL/, with the nuisances of config/Datacards/SL/systematics/fakes.yaml).

    Choosing which variables to histogram¶

    As for any FLAF analysis, the variable set is controlled by the variables: list in user_custom.yaml (or --variables). A short list keeps test runs fast:

    variables:
  - lep1_pt
  - ggF_DNN_HH

    Control plots¶

    HistPlotTask draws the stacked control plots with the HH→bb̄WW settings below; the keys are FLAF's, described in its HistPlotTask reference.

    • Backgrounds are grouped into one stack entry each (plot_process_groups in config/global.yaml), from the bottom: Diboson + Triboson, Single Higgs, W→ℓν + Jets, DY (or, with Run3_Model_DYSplit, DY m_ℓℓ ∈ [10, 50] GeV and DY m_ℓℓ > 50 GeV), Single top, t̄t + t̄tV.
    • Signal: only the spin-0 (Radion) X→HH signal is drawn, at M_X = 400, 700 and 1000 GeV, in every channel of the eras with these samples (Run3_2022 to Run3_2023BPix, and Run3_22_23): per mass, one curve summing its bb̄WW dilepton (which includes HH→bb̄ZZ→2ℓ2ν), bb̄WW single-lepton and bb̄ττ samples. Each sample is normalised to σ(X→HH) = 1 pb times the branching fraction of its own final state, and the final states do not overlap. The merged histograms keep the samples apart (see Production model). Signals are normalised to the total background (signal_plot_scale: bkg), as the legend says.
    • Control regions (plot_control_regions: [ TT_CR, DY_CR ]): no signal is drawn, and the data is shown even for variables blinded in the signal regions (blind: true in config/plot/histograms.yaml).
    • Binary DNNs of the SL windows (TwoStageDNN_M<MX>_binary, 250–1500 GeV): data hidden above a threshold per mass, region, channel and category (blind_ranges, resolved per plot as FLAF resolves x_rebin). Each mass m′ has a reference per spin and channel: 0.2 × its largest s/√b (b = TT) over the fit bins of the three categories of that channel in its own HME window (the SR binning of the SL datacards, the score flows in the first and last bin as there). In each signal-like region of the score of MX — its HME window SR_SL_HMESub_m<MX>, the HME side bins of SR_SL (SR_SL_HMESB*_m<MX>) and SR_SL itself — per spin, channel and category, in the fit bins of the window of MX, the data are shown from the lowest score up to the first bin where s/√b of any of the 25 masses reaches that mass's reference (same spin and channel; a bin without TT holding any signal reaches every reference), and hidden from that bin upwards; per channel and category the lower of the two spins' edges. A plot where no bin reaches a reference is not blinded. SR_SL, which holds the window, is hidden at least from the window's threshold of the same channel and category. The inclusive category takes the lowest threshold of the channel's three categories (it also holds the resolved events whose leading b jet is not tagged, which none of them holds); a channel without a key of its own (the dilepton channels, whose SL regions are empty) the lowest of the region. Neighbouring masses often set the threshold: at low mass a wide bin with thousands of TT events reaches the reference of a neighbour although s/b is below 0.1 %, and the side bins of a mass lie where the windows of its neighbours are. The signals of the other masses come from the study model SL_CrossMassSignals (Setup). SR_SL_Fakeable and the CR_SL_* are never blinded. A region without a key of its own (those of other flavours, e.g. CI) takes the lowest threshold of the window; a CI region with an SL name takes that name's key. test/test_plot_blinding_config.py checks that the thresholds cover every channel, category and region the scores are plotted in. The binary DNNs of other masses stay blinded whole (blind: true).
    • Labels: "CMS Preliminary" ("CMS Simulation Preliminary" where the data are blinded); the luminosity of each era is the one the simulation is normalised to; axis titles carry their units.
    • Tests: test/test_plot_config.py (CI: Unit Tests, through FLAF's reusable workflow) checks, with FLAF's own plot rules, in every era, the Run3_22_23 meta-era and both DY models: the control regions (exactly TT_CR and DY_CR) with the data they show, the signal normalisation, the grouping and stack order of the backgrounds, and the X→HH entries. It does not render the plots.
